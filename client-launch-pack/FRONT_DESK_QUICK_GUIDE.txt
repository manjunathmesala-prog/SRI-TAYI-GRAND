HOW TO USE KEYCARDS AT THE FRONT DESK:

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Go to: https://sritayigrand.com/admin/rfid
3. Check the top indicator:
   - It should say: 🟢 "Hardware Online (USB Port 1)"

TO ISSUE A ROOM KEY FOR A GUEST:
1. Place a blank keycard flat on the Be-Tech card machine.
2. On the website, click "+ Issue Customer Card" (or click "Walk-in" -> "Check-in & Encode").
3. Select the Room Number (e.g. 101, 102, 201) and guest name.
4. Click "Write Physical Card".
5. The machine will beep, and the screen will say "Success".
6. Hand the keycard to the guest.

TO TEST OR READ ANY KEYCARD:
1. Place any keycard on the Be-Tech machine.
2. On the website, click "Read Physical Card".
3. The screen will immediately display which room the card belongs to and its status.

IF THE SCREEN SAYS "HARDWARE OFFLINE":
1. Make sure the USB cable from the card machine is firmly plugged into the computer.
2. Click "Recheck Connection" on the website.
3. If it still says offline, double-click the desktop icon:
   "2_TEST_RFID_HARDWARE.bat" to see what is disconnected.
